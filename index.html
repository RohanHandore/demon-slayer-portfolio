About

Support engineer who writes code

I work in a tier-1 payments estate: monitoring, troubleshooting and availability of critical, customer-facing applications, including partner services. I am one of the on-call owners on a 24x7 rotation, so I am the person who reads the logs, isolates the fault, and drives the fix with development and infrastructure teams when something breaks at an inconvenient hour.

The part I care about is what happens after the incident. Every repeat problem is a script waiting to be written, so I automate the routine work with Python and Bash, validate every fix outside production first, and write the runbook so the next event is shorter.

Before this I taught: three years of lecturing and training on data structures, algorithms and system design fundamentals, at colleges and at TechnoKraft. It is where I learned to explain hard things plainly, which turns out to be most of what good incident communication is.
